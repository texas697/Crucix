Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Create an account with your email address and a password, or continue with Google. Access may be limited to approved email addresses; if you're told your email isn't allowed, contact us.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password. We'll email you a link to set a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do AI summaries and deep-dive research work?

AI features run on your own AI provider account. Add an API key from a supported provider (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI, Mistral or OpenRouter) in Settings. Your key is encrypted on our servers, is only used for requests you make, and any usage charges are billed to you by that provider. You can remove the key at any time.

How does the feed learn what I like?

Rate pieces with thumbs up or thumbs down. Your ratings help rank similar topics and sources higher in your feed. Rate a piece again to clear your rating.

An article is missing, wrong or won't load.

Articles come from third-party publishers, which occasionally change or remove pages. Use Send feedback in the app and choose Data looks wrong or missing, or email us with the article title.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Use Send feedback in the app. You can describe the problem and choose whether to include a screenshot and diagnostic information. See our Privacy Policy for what is collected.

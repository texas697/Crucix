1. Summary

  • We collect only what is needed to run your account and the features you use: your email address, sign-in identifiers, your app settings, and the feedback and research you create in the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We do not access your device location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • If you add your own AI provider API key, it is encrypted at rest and used only to run the AI features you request.
  • You can delete your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8).

  • communicate with you about your account, security, and changes to the Service or these policies; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (providing the Service), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improving the Service), compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it (you may withdraw consent at any time).

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers. Google Cloud and Google Firebase (hosting, authentication, database and logging) process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • AI providers you choose. When you use an AI feature, the relevant content (for example, article text, headlines and map-area context) is sent to the AI provider you configured (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Mistral, OpenRouter, MiniMax, or a custom endpoint you specify), or to our provider when a shared key is used. We do not send your email address or account details with these requests. See Section 6.
  • Search providers. When you request a research report on an article, search queries derived from the article's topic (not from your personal information) are sent to news and web search services such as GDELT, Tavily, DuckDuckGo or Bing.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of us, our users or the public.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent. In any other case, only with your direction or consent.

6. AI features and your own API key

  • If you add your own API key, it is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, decrypted only in server memory at the moment a request is made, and never displayed back to you or anyone else in full.
  • Requests made with your key are subject to your agreement with, and the privacy policy of, that AI provider, and any usage charges are billed to you by that provider.
  • You can remove your key at any time in Settings; removing it also deletes your stored AI settings and saved trade ideas.
  • AI-generated content may be inaccurate. See our Terms of Service.

7. Data retention

  • Account data, settings and content you create are kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Feedback (including screenshots and diagnostics) is kept for up to 24 months so we can track recurring issues, then deleted, or sooner if you delete your account.
  • Your API key is deleted immediately when you remove it or delete your account.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period (generally no more than 30 days) unless needed longer to investigate a security incident.
  • Backups may persist for a short additional period before being overwritten.
  • We may keep limited information longer where required by law or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your {{APP_NAME}} account and all associated data at any time:

  1. In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm.
  2. By email: send a message from the email address on your account to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm the request to verify that it came from you.

When your account is deleted we remove your sign-in account, email address, AI provider settings and encrypted API key, trade ideas, article ratings, research reports, and any feedback, screenshots and diagnostics you submitted. Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request, except for limited records we must keep for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons, and short-lived backups and logs as described in Section 7. Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account.

If you only want specific data removed (for example, your API key or your ratings) without deleting your account, you can do so in the app or contact us.
